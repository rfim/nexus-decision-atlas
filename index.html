    Draw your own Nexus model

    The book ends by asking you to make your own model. For a data platform, the pattern is the same:

    1. Pick the one decision you keep having to make (which source to onboard next, which table to deprecate).
    2. Name the two forces that really drive it. Those are your axes.
    3. Plot the last ten real cases on it. If they cluster, you have a rule; write it down in the platform handbook.
    4. Re-plot every quarter. A model that never moves anything is decoration.
